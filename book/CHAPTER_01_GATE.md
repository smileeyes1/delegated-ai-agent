# Chapter 01 Gate

The chapter cannot enter drafting until all of the following are true:

- primary evidence records exist;
- each record has provenance and context;
- evidence status is explicit;
- a counter-evidence pass is complete;
- material disagreements are recorded;
- claims are mapped to evidence IDs;
- unsupported claims are marked `BLOCKED`.

Current gate: `BLOCKED_PENDING_EVIDENCE`.

This is intentional: the system must discover the answer rather than manufacture it.
