# Chapter 01 — Research Specification

## Research question
What does the Qur'an itself say revelation is intended to produce in the human being?

## Scope
Start with Qur'anic evidence only. Do not assume a thesis. Collect passages concerning guidance, faith, taqwa, purification, remembrance, worship, character, justice, mercy, responsibility, steadfastness, and reform only when the text itself establishes relevance.

## Evidence requirements
Each candidate passage must record:
- exact reference
- source text/provenance
- immediate context
- relevant concept
- confidence/status
- competing interpretation if material

## Prohibited moves
- No invented citations.
- No hadith inserted into the Qur'an-only research stage.
- No claim that a verse means a conclusion merely because it sounds compatible.
- No suppression of verses that complicate the emerging thesis.

## Output
Produce an evidence map first, then an argument map, then a bounded draft. Every substantive draft claim must trace to one or more evidence IDs.

## Gate
Status remains `RESEARCHING` until the evidence map contains sufficient primary evidence and a counter-evidence pass has been completed.
