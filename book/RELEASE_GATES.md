# Book Release Gates v1

A manuscript may be released only after all applicable gates pass.

1. Source integrity: primary texts and citations are traceable.
2. Evidence integrity: every material claim has evidence links and explicit uncertainty.
3. Counter-evidence: material contrary evidence and disagreement were checked and surfaced.
4. Reasoning integrity: inference is distinguishable from source statement.
5. Hadith integrity: hadith claims require provenance and appropriate grading/context.
6. Language/editorial quality: Arabic, structure, terminology, references, and consistency pass review.
7. Regression: prior verified claims and chapter dependencies remain intact.
8. Impact analysis: changed evidence identifies affected chapters/claims.
9. Reproducibility: the pipeline can rerun from checkpoints and produce auditable artifacts.
10. Sovereign gate: unresolved high-stakes religious conclusions are escalated rather than silently approved.

Failure at any gate blocks release and creates a repair task.
