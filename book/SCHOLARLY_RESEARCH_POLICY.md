# SCHOLARLY RESEARCH POLICY v1.0

## Evidence layers
- L0: raw primary text
- L1: verified metadata and provenance
- L2: authenticated hadith / established textual evidence
- L3: tafsir or scholarly interpretation, explicitly attributed
- L4: comparative synthesis
- L5: authorial application to contemporary life

A higher layer may not masquerade as a lower layer.

## Claim ledger rule
Every important claim must carry: claim_id, wording, evidence_refs, evidence_layer, confidence, counter_evidence, unresolved_questions, and status.

## Quran rule
Quranic text is copied from a pinned primary corpus. Search is exploratory; citation is verified against the corpus before acceptance.

## Hadith rule
No hadith enters the evidence ledger as authenticated merely because a model calls it sahih. The record must preserve collection, book/number or stable locator, Arabic/text provenance, grading source, and any relevant scholarly disagreement.

## Tafsir rule
Tafsir is explanatory evidence, not Quranic text. Different tafsir positions remain separately attributed until comparison supports synthesis.

## Counter-evidence rule
For every high-impact conclusion the system must search for: limiting verses, apparent exceptions, alternative interpretations, and known scholarly disagreement.

## Writing rule
The manuscript is downstream of the evidence ledger. Research notes may be revised freely; accepted manuscript claims require passing gates.

## Confidence vocabulary
- VERIFIED: source and claim match checked.
- WELL_SUPPORTED: multiple compatible evidence items, no material unresolved conflict.
- PLAUSIBLE: reasonable inference, but not directly established.
- OPEN: evidence insufficient or materially contested.
- REJECTED: contradicted, unsupported, or provenance failed.

Never use certainty language stronger than the assigned confidence.
