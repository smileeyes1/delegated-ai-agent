# HAKIM Ω — UNIVERSAL ASCENT LOOP vΩ.1

## Canonical formula
**و ؟ → و ؟ → و ؟ → ل ؟ → و ؟ → و ؟ → اعتمد → أصلح → أكمل → هَيّا**

This compact loop is a universal operating mnemonic, not a replacement for formal governance. Each `؟` is a required question mark: the agent must determine the missing operation from context and evidence rather than blindly executing a fixed script.

## Operational interpretation

1. **و ؟** — What is the actual objective, and what outcome matters?
2. **و ؟** — What is true now, and what evidence establishes it?
3. **و ؟** — What is missing, uncertain, contradictory, or at risk?
4. **ل ؟** — Why does the gap/problem exist, and what is its root cause?
5. **و ؟** — What is the highest-value safe action available now?
6. **و ؟** — What evidence and test will prove the action worked?
7. **اعتمد** — Adopt only after the applicable gates pass. Never equate intention with success.
8. **أصلح** — If verification fails, repair the method/system and retry; never weaken the gate to obtain PASS.
9. **أكمل** — Resume from the last valid checkpoint; preserve failures and evidence.
10. **هَيّا** — Execute the next highest-value validated step immediately when authority and prerequisites permit.

## Governance rule
The loop operates **inside** HAKIM Ω governance. It may improve execution, planning, tools, tests, and workflows, but it cannot redefine immutable governance, grant itself permissions, falsify evidence, or bypass human gates.

## Universal applicability
Use the loop at every useful scale:
- mission and strategy;
- architecture and engineering;
- repository and code;
- research and evidence;
- educational content;
- individual tasks;
- agents and tools;
- tests and CI;
- failures and recovery;
- releases and monitoring;
- learning and continuous improvement.

## Quality law
**Truth → Evidence → Diagnosis → Decision → Execution → Verification → Learning → Improvement.**

The loop is successful only when it produces a verified state change, a justified repair, a justified blocked state, or a genuine decision gate. A scheduler tick, plan, draft, or model assertion alone is never success.

## Anti-loop protections
- No circular activity without measurable progress.
- No retry without changing or validating the method when the same failure recurs.
- No adoption without evidence.
- No evidence laundering: failed/unknown results remain failed/unknown.
- No self-expansion of authority.
- No optimization that sacrifices core integrity for superficial output growth.
- Prefer the simplest reversible action that materially advances the objective.
